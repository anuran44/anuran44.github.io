About Me

I am a Computer Science researcher specializing in representation learning, verifiable natural language processing, and imitation learning. I have a proven capability in engineering deterministic, physically-grounded algorithmic architectures and bypassing heavy framework abstractions to design custom neural typologies from scratch.

My research is deeply focused on advancing scalable, trustworthy AI systems that bridge theoretical mathematical foundations with high-stakes, real-world deployment, directly aligning with decision-making and imitation paradigms in modern machine learning.

During my academic journey at the University of Calcutta, I have had the privilege of collaborating with esteemed researchers. My thesis work was conducted under the supervision of Prof. S. K. Setua (Click to view research profile).

Advanced Research & Projects

PHORENSICS: Physics Forensics Engine

Python Docker PySpark

Published the core algorithmic framework to PyPI to ensure deterministic, reproducible computational outcomes. Architected pure Python routines for Global Spectral Analysis to mathematically isolate Generative AI origins beyond standard semantic AI limits. Engineered dynamic VRAM tracking and automatic CPU/GPU hot-swapping to prevent OOM tensor crashes within a distributed PySpark Docker cluster.

AI-Powered Fact Verification System

Python LLMs Sentence Transformers

Developed a hybrid verification framework combining local Gemma3 LLM reasoning, semantic tracking, and iterative self-improvement loops to validate factual claims. Implemented an adaptive patience-based convergence mechanism. Achieved state-of-the-art performance against the FEVER baseline, improving Precision by +8% and F1-Score by +10%.

Designed a custom Convolutional Siamese Network utilizing Contrastive Loss to force topological embedding separation. Hooked asynchronous UI rendering directly into the PyTorch epoch loop for live telemetry updates.

End-to-End Autonomous Vehicle Navigation
