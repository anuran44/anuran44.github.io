Anuran Bhattacharya

About Me

Hi! I'm a Master of Technology student in Computer Science & Engineering at the University of Calcutta where I am advised by Prof. S. K. Setua.

My research interests generally lie in representation learning, verifiable natural language processing, and imitation learning. During my current Master's, I primarily focused on verifiable AI and computational forensics for synthetic media, and during my previous Master's and undergrad, I worked on developing resource-scarce neural networks for computational linguistics and predictive analytics for real-estate datasets.

I previously completed my Master of Science at the University of Calcutta and my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at The Bhawanipur Education Society College.

Advanced Research & Projects

PHORENSICS: Physics Forensics Engine

Python Docker PySpark

Published the core algorithmic framework to PyPI to ensure deterministic, reproducible computational outcomes. Architected pure Python routines for Global Spectral Analysis to mathematically isolate Generative AI origins beyond standard semantic AI limits. Engineered dynamic VRAM tracking and automatic CPU/GPU hot-swapping to prevent OOM tensor crashes within a distributed PySpark Docker cluster.

AI-Powered Fact Verification System

Python LLMs Sentence Transformers

Developed a hybrid verification framework combining local Gemma3 LLM reasoning, semantic tracking, and iterative self-improvement loops to validate factual claims. Implemented an adaptive patience-based convergence mechanism. Achieved state-of-the-art performance against the FEVER baseline, improving Precision by +8% and F1-Score by +10%.

TxtVis: Multimodal Latent Space Representation

PyTorch Metric Learning Streamlit

Engineered a "Tabula Rasa" dual-path PyTorch research pipeline for extracting and visualizing high-dimensional embeddings. Designed a custom Convolutional Siamese Network utilizing Contrastive Loss to force topological embedding separation. Hooked asynchronous UI rendering directly into the PyTorch epoch loop for live telemetry updates.

End-to-End Autonomous Vehicle Navigation

Python OpenCV Flask

Engineered an end-to-end behavioral cloning architecture based on the NVIDIA DAVE-2 CNN to learn complex perception-to-control mapping. Developed robust data preprocessing and augmentation engines. Architected a bidirectional closed-loop client-server environment to stream concurrent real-time telemetry and execute predictive steering control.

Accommodation Rent Prediction

CatBoost Scikit-Learn

Modeled highly nonlinear relationships within structured real-estate datasets using specialized CatBoost regression, mitigating overfitting through ordered boosting. Trained computationally efficient, independent localized regression models to preserve regional micro-economic variance.
