I am an M.Tech. graduate in Computer Science & Engineering dedicated to building principled, human-centered AI. My research sits precisely at the intersection of Social Computing, Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), and Natural Language Processing (NLP).

Research Vision

In my doctoral research, I aim to advance Trustworthy AI by unifying NLP, Computer Vision, and HCI under a single human-centered framework. My goal is to design interactive, verifiable systems that empower the public to co-reason with generative models—building computational defenses that safeguard society against multi-modal digital deception and improve reliability in health and social ecosystems.

Selected Research

PHORENSICS: Physics-Inspired Forensics

Focus: Social AI & Computer Vision

A deterministic, multi-modal unbiased forensics framework that replaces probabilistic black-box ML models. It establishes mathematical boundaries between naturally captured photons and artificially generated pixels to combat deepfakes and synthetic media for public benefit.

Code

Adaptive Social AI Fact Verification

Focus: HCI & NLP Alignment

Developed a retrieval-augmented fact verification framework combining Gemma 3 LLM reasoning with semantic document retrieval. To prevent human cognitive overload, I proposed an adaptive convergence strategy that terminates retrieval once semantic similarity stabilizes, ensuring concise claim-evidence pairing.

Code

BEN-RS-ANN: Bengali Emotion Detection

Focus: NLP & Speech Processing

Developed a resource-scarce Artificial Neural Network (RS-ANN) designed for emotion detection in Bengali text. This framework specifically targets polysemy resolution (words with multiple meanings) to improve context understanding in human-language environments.
